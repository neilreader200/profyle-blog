University Staff Headshots: A Simple Guide for Academic and Professional Services Teams

2026-06-08

University profile photos have to do more than fill a square on a staff directory. They support trust, recognition and credibility across research pages, departmental websites, conference programmes, LinkedIn and student-facing communications.

The challenge is that universities are often large, decentralised and hybrid. That makes consistent headshots surprisingly hard.

Who needs a university headshot?

Professional headshots are useful for:

  • academics and lecturers
  • researchers and PhD supervisors
  • professional services staff
  • admissions and outreach teams
  • senior leadership
  • careers and employability teams
  • press, media and public-engagement profiles

The image does not need to be formal in an old-fashioned way. It needs to be recognisable, current and appropriate for the audience.

Why university headshots become inconsistent

Most university teams inherit a mix of image sources:

  • old staff-directory photos
  • conference photos
  • cropped group shots
  • selfies
  • press images
  • photos from previous institutions
  • blank placeholders

That inconsistency can make a department page feel less polished than the work behind it.

What a strong academic headshot looks like

For most academic and university staff profiles, the best headshot is:

  • well-lit
  • cleanly cropped
  • recent
  • approachable
  • not over-retouched
  • consistent enough to sit beside colleagues' images

The point is not vanity. It is professional clarity.

AI headshots for university teams

AI headshots can help when teams are spread across campuses, working hybrid patterns, or adding new starters throughout the year. They reduce the need for repeated photography sessions and can make profiles more consistent.

For individuals, they are also useful when a new role, grant, conference or media appearance needs a better image quickly.
